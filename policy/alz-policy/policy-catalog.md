- **149** custom policy definitions imported from ALZ: **93** active and **56** deprecated upstream. Deprecated ones are published only with `-IncludeDeprecated`.
- **1** local definition(s) authored in this repository (`local-definitions/`), not imported from ALZ: `Audit-RHEL-Hybrid-Benefit`. They audit by default like the others, are part of the initiative and are never touched by the import.
- Every imported definition is converted to audit by default. **137** of them (**81** active) applied an enforcing effect upstream (Deny, DeployIfNotExists, Modify, Append or DenyAction); the others already audited. The **Default** column is the effect that applies unless you choose another one, both when a definition is assigned on its own and through the initiative. The upstream effect stays selectable, is shown as **Upstream default** and is recorded in each definition's `metadata.alzUpstreamEffect`. Every policy also accepts `Disabled`.
- To default to audit, **77** of the 149 imported definitions got an audit-mode effect added to their allowed values and **5** turned a hard-coded effect into a parameter.
- **1** imported policy rule(s) are adjusted compared to upstream (see [Adjusted policy rules](#adjusted-policy-rules)); every other policy rule is upstream's.
- Effect parameter name: the policy name with every non-alphanumeric character replaced by `_`, followed by `_effect`.
- The roles column lists what the assignment identity needs when the policy is switched to a deploying or modifying effect.
